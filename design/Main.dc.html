HOW IT WORKS

Mint. Trade. Settle.

One contract, two tokens, no margin. The whole payout is locked in before anyone trades.

01

Mint

Deposit USDC equal to the most the market can ever pay. You get one LONG and one SHORT token for every GPU-hour.

Deposit350.00 USDC
100 LONG 100 SHORT
02

Trade

Sell the side you don't want. Renting GPUs and fear a rise? Keep LONG. Renting them out and fear a fall? Keep SHORT.

LONGpays if H100 rises
SHORTpays if H100 falls
03

Settle

At expiry the TEE oracle writes the attested index. The contract splits the collateral between the two sides. That's it.
