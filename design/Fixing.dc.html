The price, and the proof.

Every hour an enclave reads public GPU prices, takes the median and signs it. The contract only accepts a fixing with a valid attestation.

01 · READ 4 public sources Akash, RunPod, Vast.ai, Shadeform. Live H100 listings.
02 · COMPUTE IN A TEE Median of medians One median per provider, then the median across them. Nobody can edit it mid-run.
03 · ATTEST Signed by the enclave The proof names the exact code that ran and the inputs it saw.
04 · WRITE ON NEAR Checked, then stored The contract verifies the proof before a fixing can settle a market.
